Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking a Cape Town airport shuttle or private tour with Blaqrock.

Do you offer Cape Town International Airport transfers?

Yes. We collect and drop off at Cape Town International Airport 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, from Hout Bay, the City Bowl, the Atlantic Seaboard, the Southern Suburbs, the Winelands and anywhere else in Cape Town.

How do I book a shuttle or tour with Blaqrock?

Send us a WhatsApp on +27 79 261 8265, call +27 61 326 9699, email bookings@blaqrockshuttle.co.za, or fill in the booking form below. Steve, your host, will confirm your booking and quote.

What vehicle will I travel in?

Our flagship vehicle is a spacious white Toyota Fortuner SUV with tinted windows, air conditioning, generous luggage space and a tow bar for an extra luggage trailer — comfortable for up to 6 passengers.

Which private tours do you offer around Cape Town?

We create custom private tours including Chapman's Peak Drive, Cape Point and the Cape Peninsula, Table Mountain, Boulders Beach and the Stellenbosch and Franschhoek Winelands. Tell us what you'd like to see and we'll plan the day around you.

Do you travel outside Cape Town?

Yes. We are based in Hout Bay, Cape Town, and also do long-distance transfers and tours across the Western Cape and the rest of South Africa on request.
